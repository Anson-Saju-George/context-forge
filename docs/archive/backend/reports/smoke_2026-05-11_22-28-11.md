Implementation and operational comparison:

- Transformer architecture supplies the model mechanism: self-attention, multi-head attention, scaled dot-product attention, encoder/decoder processing, and sequence generation.
- Llama 2 builds on the Transformer family as an autoregressive foundation model, then adds production-relevant training and alignment stages such as pretraining, fine-tuning, RLHF, PPO, and rejection sampling.
- RAG sits above the model layer. It retrieves external evidence with mechanisms such as DPR, top-k retrieval, MIPS, RAG-sequence, and RAG-token, then conditions generation on retrieved context.
- Kubernetes is the operational layer. It manages containerized AI services through control-plane orchestration, kube-apiserver, etcd, kubelet, scheduling, reconciliation, services, deployments, and load balancing.
